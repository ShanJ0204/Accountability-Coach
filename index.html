The unit economics

Why these prices work.

Each interaction has a real cost — an AI vision/coaching call plus a WhatsApp message fee. Pricing is set so an active subscriber's monthly cost stays a small fraction of their plan.

~$0.02est. AI cost per food-photo analysis (Claude Opus 4.8, structured output)
~$2–5est. blended AI + messaging cost / active user / month
60–80%target gross margin on the Pro tier at typical usage

Estimates for planning only. Switch the model to Claude Sonnet 5 (a one-line config change) to cut per-call cost ~40% if margins tighten at scale. Actual costs depend on image size, usage frequency, and your WhatsApp/Twilio rate.

Go-to-market

Steps to monetize.

A practical sequence from first users to durable revenue.

1 Launch free, meter the value

  • Ship the free tier to remove friction — the habit is the hook.
  • Cap the expensive action (photo logs/day) to protect margins.
  • Instrument logs/day, retention, and photo-vs-text usage from day one.

2 Convert with a trial

  • Offer a 7-day Pro trial triggered after a user hits the free photo cap.
  • Add a paywall message in-chat: "You've logged 3 today — go unlimited for $9.99/mo."
  • Bill via Stripe Payment Links or WhatsApp Pay; store plan on the user row.

3 Raise ARPU with tiers & annual

  • Push annual plans (save ~2 months) to lift LTV and smooth cash flow.
  • Coach+ adds plans + a human touch — higher price, higher retention.
  • Add-ons: extra family members, a 1:1 human coach seat.

4 Grow beyond direct subscriptions

  • B2B / white-label: license to gyms, trainers, and clinics per seat.
  • Referrals: give a free month for each friend who subscribes.
  • Affiliates: recommend meal kits / supplements with disclosed partner links.
  • Corporate wellness: per-employee plans sold to HR teams.
